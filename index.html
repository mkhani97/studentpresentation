Site decisions · Judgment calls

Building the right things, fast

SCOPE CUT

Dropped the Schedule skill

Adding tasks by chat hit platform limits and would have saved PMs little time. I recommended cutting it and putting the effort into status reports and risks, where the time savings were.

Measured the slowdown

10+ minWEEKDAY, BUSINESS HOURS
5 min 23 sSAME REQUEST, WEEKEND

Same agent, same request. The cause was capacity under load, not a broken design, which pointed to targeted fixes.

Site rules · What I learned

Four lessons I’m taking with me

RULE 01

Data first

Every app and agent was only as good as the database under it.

RULE 02

Guarantee the important parts

Let the AI draft and talk, but send every save through a fixed workflow.

RULE 03

Saying no is part of the job

Cutting a low-value feature freed time for work PMs actually needed.

RULE 04

Measure before fixing

One timed test changed the whole conversation about performance.
